*LMS Portal* = *Learning Management System Portal*

It’s basically an online website/app where students and teachers manage all school/college/course work in one place.

### *What you can do on an LMS Portal:*
1. *Classes & Courses* - See your subjects, syllabus, and timetable
2. *Study Material* - Download notes, PDFs, videos, online classes,  presentations uploaded by teachers
3. *Assignments & Tests* - Submit homework, quizzes, and get grades
4. *Attendance* - Check your class attendance record incase of online classes
5. *Announcements* - Get notices, exam dates, and updates from teachers/admin
6. *Discussion/Chat* - Ask questions and talk with teachers
7. *Results* - View marksheets and progress reports

### *Examples of LMS Portals:*
- *Google Classroom*
- *Moodle*
- *Canvas*
- *Blackboard*
- University/School specific ones like _VU LMS_, _LMS NUML_, _LMS COMSATS_, etc.
